# Protocol: Dual-Gate Response
- Objective: Standardized, fail-closed reporting protocol.
- Sections:
  1. [Task]: The objective of the ingestion or analysis cycle.
  2. [Data]: JSON-formatted payloads representing ingestion metrics or governance principles.
  3. [Evidence]: Verification artifacts, test output summaries, or audit logs.
  4. [Residual Debt]: Unresolved blockers or outstanding hardening items.
